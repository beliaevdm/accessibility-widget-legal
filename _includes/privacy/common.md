## 7. Information we receive about site owners

## 8. Why we use information, and the legal basis

- **To provide the app** and the features of your plan, and to answer your support requests — performance of our
  contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To keep the app working and safe** — to prevent abuse (for example the spam limits described in section 3),
  fix errors and decide what to improve — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). For this we look at
  installation-level figures (installs, plans), not at profiles of individual people.
- **To keep records required by tax and accounting law** — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Information about visitors is processed only to run the features the site owner turned on, on the site owner's
instructions; the site owner decides the legal basis for it.

We **do not sell** personal information, **do not share it** for cross-context behavioural advertising, do not
build advertising profiles and do not use visitors' information for our own purposes. We make no decisions with
legal or similarly significant effect based solely on automated processing.

## 11. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Owner-only actions are checked against the Wix login of the site's owner or its
collaborators. Where an app has to recognise repeat submissions, it stores one-way hashes, not network addresses.
Each feature collects only what it needs. If we learn of a security incident affecting your data, we inform Wix
and the affected site owners without undue delay.
