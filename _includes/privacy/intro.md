## 2. Who this policy is for, and our role

The policy explains what information {% if page.hub %}the apps handle{% else %}the app handles{% endif %}, why, where it is kept and what choices you have. It
concerns two groups of people:

- **Site owners** — Wix users who install the app on their site (including people the owner lets manage the site).
  For information about you as our customer — your installation, plan and the messages you send us — we decide how
  it is used and act as an independent controller. Wix is a separate controller for your Wix account.
- **Site visitors** — people who visit a site where the app is installed. Whatever the app handles about visitors,
  it handles on behalf of the site owner and only as the owner's settings say. The site owner is the controller of
  that information and we act as their processor. If the app collects visitors' information on your site (section 3
  says whether it does), mention it in your own site's privacy policy. Visitors who have questions about their data
  should contact the site owner first; we help site owners answer such requests.
