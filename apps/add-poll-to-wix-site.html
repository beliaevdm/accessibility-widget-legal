How to add a poll to your Wix website

Ask visitors a question and show live results, without code.

Steps

  1. Install Polls & Surveys from the Wix App Market.
  2. Open the Polls page in your site dashboard and create a poll (question and options).
  3. In the Editor, add the Polls widget to a page and pick your poll in its settings.
  4. Publish. Visitors vote, and results update live.

Poll types

  • Single choice and multiple choice
  • Star rating
  • Image polls
  • Ranking
  • Head-to-head (two options)

What you get

  • Analytics per poll and CSV export of results.
  • Optional thank-you message, redirect, and email capture after voting.
  • Webhook so votes and leads can flow into tools like Zapier or Make.
  • Dark mode and colour presets; custom themes on the Premium plan.
