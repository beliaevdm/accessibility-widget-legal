A game launch countdown for your store or community

Big game releases unlock at midnight in each country. Game stores, gaming cafés, clubs and streamers can count down the same way on their own site.

This autumn’s biggest example is Grand Theft Auto VI, scheduled by its publisher for 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Launch Countdown is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive; game names are used only to describe the release date. Use your own artwork, not the publisher’s logos or key art.

Set it up for launch night

  • Start from the Game launch night template: it already uses “local time for every visitor”, so a fan in Tokyo and one in New York both count down to their own midnight.
  • Write your own headline (“Midnight launch at our store”), point the button to your pre-order page, and add the time your doors open.
  • Pick the Neon sunset theme, or Arcade and Night drive on Premium.
  • At zero, switch the button to “Buy now” or send visitors to your product page; turn on confetti for the moment it hits zero.
  • Leave “Add to calendar” on, so visitors get a reminder for launch night.

Questions

Can I use the game’s logo or cover art?

Only if you have permission. Titles, logos and key art belong to their publishers; the built-in themes are original designs you can use freely.

What if the release date moves?

Change the date in the dashboard; every page showing the countdown updates at once.
