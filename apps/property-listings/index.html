Property Listings & Search for Wix

Show your homes, flats, land and rentals on any page of a Wix site, let visitors search and filter them, and receive enquiries about each property in your dashboard.

What it does

  • Four layouts: card grid, list, a large showcase and a carousel.
  • Search by address or reference; filter by sale or rent, type, location, price range, bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • Each property opens with a photo gallery, description, features, facts, a map link and its own shareable link.
  • An enquiry form under every property: enquiries are kept in a dashboard inbox and you are notified of each one.
  • Call, WhatsApp and email buttons with your own numbers.
  • One property library for the whole site; each board can show only rentals, one area, one type or one tag.
  • Paste many properties at once from Google Sheets or Excel; export everything as CSV.
  • Six themes, any currency and number style, editable wording for any language and keyboard access.

Questions

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live board with up to 12 properties, the grid and list layouts, three themes, search and every filter, the photo gallery, the enquiry form with notifications, contact buttons, spreadsheet import and CSV export. Premium adds unlimited boards, up to 500 properties per board, showcase and carousel layouts, all themes, your colors and font, video and virtual tour buttons, a printable property sheet, and removes the credit line.

Does it connect to MLS or IDX feeds?

No. Properties are added in the app dashboard or pasted from a spreadsheet. MLS and IDX feeds need separate credentials and services.

Will removing the widget delete my properties?

No. Properties and enquiries are kept in their own library, separate from the boards and widgets that show them.

Where do enquiries go?

Into the Inbox tab of the app dashboard, and you get a Wix notification for each new one. They can be exported as CSV.
