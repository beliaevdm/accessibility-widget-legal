How to add a quiz to a Wix site

A quiz is one of the easiest ways to make a page interactive. Here is how to add one to a Wix site without code or HTML embeds.

1. Install Quiz Maker

Install Quiz Maker from the Wix App Market. The app opens in your site dashboard with a ready-made personality quiz that is already live, so you can see a finished example straight away.

2. Pick or edit a quiz

Choose one of eight templates or start blank. Change questions, answers and results in the editor; the live preview on the right shows exactly what visitors will see. Add images from your media library if you like.

3. Put it on a page

Open the site in the Wix editor, click Add Elements → App widgets and add Quiz Maker to the page. In the widget settings pick the quiz to show, then publish the site.

4. Collect leads (optional)

In the quiz settings, turn on the lead form before or after the result. Names and emails appear in the app dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Why use an app instead of an HTML embed?

An embedded quiz from another website lives in an iframe that often has a fixed height and does not follow your site design. Quiz Maker runs as a native widget, follows the width of the page, has light and dark themes, and keeps leads inside your Wix account.

Questions

Can I show more than one quiz?

Yes. Add a widget for each quiz and pick a different quiz in each widget's settings. The Free plan keeps two quizzes live at a time.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes two live quizzes with up to ten questions each, unlimited plays and leads, all templates and themes, share buttons and Wix contacts sync. Premium adds more quizzes and questions, a question timer, CSV export, webhooks and custom colors.

Where do the leads go?

Each name and email is saved in the app dashboard with the visitor's result and added to your Wix contacts. On Premium you can export them to CSV or send them to Zapier or Make with a webhook.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The quiz shows one question at a time, adapts to the width of the page and has large touch-friendly answers.
