A Halloween quiz for your Wix site

October is the best month of the year for quizzes: people share spooky results with friends. Two ready-made Halloween quizzes take a few minutes to put on a Wix page.

Two templates to start from

  • Spooky Halloween trivia 🎃 — six questions about Samhain, carved turnips, Dracula and Frankenstein, with a fun fact after each answer and three score levels from “Trick-or-Treater” to “Halloween Legend”.
  • Which Halloween costume fits you? 👻 — a personality quiz with four results (Vampire, Witch, Superhero, Zombie). Add a button to each result that links to a matching costume or party ticket.

Ideas that work for businesses

  • Cafés and bakeries: run the trivia on your site and offer the Halloween special to people who leave their email.
  • Party and costume shops: use the costume quiz as a product finder; turn on the automatic redirect so visitors land on the right costume.
  • Schools and libraries: share the trivia in your newsletter; visitors see the right answer and a short fact after every question.

How to add it

Install Quiz Maker from the Wix App Market, choose a Halloween template, edit anything you like, then add the Quiz Maker widget to a page in the Wix editor and publish. Share buttons for Facebook, X and WhatsApp are built in.

Questions

Is the Halloween quiz free?

Yes. Both Halloween templates work on the Free plan, which includes two live quizzes with up to ten questions and unlimited plays.

Can I translate it?

Yes. Pick one of eight languages (English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish) in the Texts tab and every button and message is translated; you edit the questions in the same language.

Can it collect emails for a Halloween promotion?

Yes. Turn on the lead form before or after the result. Names and emails go to the app dashboard and to your Wix contacts.
