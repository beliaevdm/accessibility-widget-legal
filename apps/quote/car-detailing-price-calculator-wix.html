Pricing tips for auto businesses

  • Start from a package price and adjust for vehicle size with a fixed amount per size.
  • Use a condition option such as "dirty" or "very dirty" with a percentage uplift.
  • Offer a mobile-service fee as a yes/no option so on-site visits are priced separately.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the car detailing template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can I add a travel fee for mobile service?

Yes. Add a Yes/No switch with a fixed amount, or a number field for miles with a price per unit.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
