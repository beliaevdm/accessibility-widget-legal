Catering quote calculator for Wix

Price events per guest from menu, drinks, staff and equipment, and collect enquiries with the guest count and choices included.

Built for: Caterers, event planners, food trucks and venues that price per head.

What your visitors answer

  • Event type: Party / birthday, Corporate lunch, Wedding (service fee), Cocktail reception
  • Guests (guests)
  • Menu: Buffet, Plated dinner, Canapés, BBQ
  • Drinks: None, Soft drinks & water, Beer & wine, Open bar
  • Serving staff
  • Equipment & extras: Tables & chairs, Linens, Glassware, Table decor

How the sample prices work

The catering template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Event type: Wedding (service fee) adds $250
  • Menu: Buffet adds $28 per guests; Plated dinner adds $46 per guests; Canapés adds $24 per guests; BBQ adds $32 per guests
  • Drinks: Soft drinks & water adds $3.50 per guests; Beer & wine adds $14 per guests; Open bar adds $26 per guests
  • Serving staff: $120 per unit
  • Equipment & extras: Tables & chairs adds $4.50 per guests; Linens adds $2 per guests; Glassware adds $2.50 per guests; Table decor adds $300
  • Minimum total: $500
  • Shown as a range of ±10%

Pricing tips for events businesses

  • Charge menus and drinks per guest. The total then follows the guest count automatically.
  • Add staffing as a per-person option and equipment as per-guest add-ons.
  • Set a minimum so small events still cover the cost of setting up.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the catering template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can I price drinks per guest?

Yes. Each drinks option can add an amount per guest, for example 14 per guest for beer and wine.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
