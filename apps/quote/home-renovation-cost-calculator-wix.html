How the sample prices work

The home renovation template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Project management: $500
  • Which room?: Bathroom adds $95 per sq ft; Kitchen adds $140 per sq ft; Basement finish adds $55 per sq ft
  • Finish level: Budget subtracts 10%; Premium adds 30%
  • Demolition & haul-away: adds $1,200
  • Move plumbing / fixtures: adds $1,800
  • Electrical upgrade: adds $1,400
  • Permits & inspections: adds $650
  • Minimum total: $2,000
  • Shown as a range of ±20%

Pricing tips for construction businesses

  • Price per square foot by room type and adjust with a finish level so budget and premium projects differ.
  • List plumbing, electrical, demolition and permits as separate options so clients understand the range.
  • Use a wide price range, around 20%, and a project-management fee to cover your own coordination time.

Questions

Is the price binding?

No. The calculator shows an estimate. You decide the final quote after seeing the project.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
