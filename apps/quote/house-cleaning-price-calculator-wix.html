House cleaning price calculator for Wix

Let visitors price a clean by home size, type of clean and how often, then send you the request with the numbers attached.

Built for: Residential and commercial cleaners, maid services and move-out cleaning crews who answer the same "how much?" question every day.

What your visitors answer

  • Home size (sq ft)
  • Type of clean: Standard clean, Deep clean, Move-in / move-out
  • Bathrooms
  • Extras: Inside oven, Inside fridge, Interior windows, Laundry (wash & fold)
  • I have pets
  • How often?: One-time, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly

How the sample prices work

The house cleaning template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Type of clean: Standard clean adds $0.10 per sq ft; Deep clean adds $0.18 per sq ft; Move-in / move-out adds $0.22 per sq ft
  • Bathrooms: $20 per unit
  • Extras: Inside oven adds $35; Inside fridge adds $30; Interior windows adds $45; Laundry (wash & fold) adds $25
  • I have pets: adds $15
  • How often?: Weekly subtracts 15%; Every 2 weeks subtracts 10%; Monthly subtracts 5%
  • Minimum total: $90

Pricing tips for cleaning businesses

  • Charge by square foot for the base clean and by the item for extras such as ovens and windows. Visitors understand extras and they raise the average job value.
  • Offer a visible discount for recurring visits. A weekly or fortnightly discount is easy to show as a percentage option and encourages repeat bookings.
  • Set a minimum charge so very small homes still cover travel and set-up time.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the house cleaning template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can I charge more for pets or heavily soiled homes?

Yes. Add a Yes/No switch with a fixed amount or a percentage, or a dropdown for "condition" with a percentage on each option.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
