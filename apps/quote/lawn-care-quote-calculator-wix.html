Lawn care and landscaping quote calculator for Wix

Price lawn services by lawn area, services chosen, hedges, terrain and visit frequency.

Built for: Lawn care companies, gardeners and landscapers who price by area.

What your visitors answer

  • Lawn area (sq ft)
  • Services: Mowing, Edging, Fertilizing, Leaf cleanup
  • Hedges / shrubs to trim
  • Terrain: Flat, Sloped, Overgrown
  • Schedule: One-time, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly

How the sample prices work

The lawn & landscaping template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Service call: $35
  • Services: Mowing adds $0.01 per sq ft; Edging adds $0.00 per sq ft; Fertilizing adds $0.01 per sq ft; Leaf cleanup adds $0.02 per sq ft
  • Hedges / shrubs to trim: $12 per unit
  • Terrain: Sloped adds 15%; Overgrown adds 30%
  • Schedule: Weekly subtracts 20%; Every 2 weeks subtracts 12%; Monthly subtracts 5%
  • Minimum total: $75

Pricing tips for landscaping businesses

  • Price services per square foot and let customers tick the services they want. The total moves as they choose.
  • Add a schedule discount for weekly or fortnightly visits to win recurring work.
  • Use a terrain option for slopes and overgrown lots, because they take longer than a flat, tidy lawn.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the lawn & landscaping template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can I price per square foot?

Yes. A choice can add an amount multiplied by the lawn-area field, for example 0.012 per square foot for mowing.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
