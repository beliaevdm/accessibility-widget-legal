Moving cost calculator for Wix

Give movers' customers a ballpark price from home size, distance and extras, and receive a request with the full breakdown.

Built for: Local and long-distance moving companies, man-and-van services and storage providers.

What your visitors answer

  • Home size: Studio, 1 bedroom, 2 bedrooms, 3 bedrooms, 4+ bedrooms
  • Distance (miles)
  • Stairs at either address: No stairs, Walk-up, 2nd floor, 3rd floor or higher
  • Extra services: Packing service, Packing supplies, Piano or heavy item, Storage, 1 month
  • Weekend or holiday date

How the sample prices work

The moving company template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Truck & crew: $120
  • Home size: Studio adds $250; 1 bedroom adds $400; 2 bedrooms adds $650; 3 bedrooms adds $950; 4+ bedrooms adds $1,350
  • Distance: graduated tiers, $1.50 up to 30, $2 up to 200, $2.80 above
  • Stairs at either address: Walk-up, 2nd floor adds $60; 3rd floor or higher adds $120
  • Extra services: Packing service adds $180; Packing supplies adds $75; Piano or heavy item adds $220; Storage, 1 month adds $150
  • Weekend or holiday date: adds 10%
  • Minimum total: $350
  • Shown as a range of ±10%

Pricing tips for moving businesses

  • Use graduated distance pricing: the first miles at a local rate and the long-distance miles at a different rate, so a 300-mile move is not priced like a local one.
  • Show a price range rather than one number. Moves vary and a range of around 10% sets the right expectation before the call.
  • Offer packing, supplies, heavy items and storage as add-ons so customers see what the base price excludes.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the moving company template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can I show a range instead of an exact price?

Yes. Set the price range percentage in the Pricing tab. The calculator then shows a minimum and a maximum around the total.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
