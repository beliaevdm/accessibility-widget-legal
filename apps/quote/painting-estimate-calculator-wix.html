Painting estimate calculator for Wix

Estimate interior painting jobs from the number of rooms, ceilings, trim, wall condition and paint quality.

Built for: Painters, decorators and handyman businesses that quote room by room.

How the sample prices work

The interior painting template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Rooms to paint: $280 per unit
  • Ceiling height: High (10 ft or more) adds 15%
  • Paint the ceilings too: adds $90 per rooms to paint
  • Trim & baseboards: adds $60 per rooms to paint
  • Wall condition: Light patching adds $120; Heavy repair adds $350
  • Paint quality: Premium (washable, low odor) adds $45 per rooms to paint
  • Minimum total: $400
  • Shown as a range of ±10%

Pricing tips for painting businesses

  • Price per room for the base job and add ceilings and trim as per-room options. That mirrors how most painters think about the job.
  • Add a separate option for wall repair so customers with damaged walls see why the price moves.
  • Round the total to the nearest 10 or 50. Painting estimates are never exact and round numbers look more honest.

Questions

Can an option depend on the number of rooms?

Yes. An option can add an amount multiplied by another field, for example 90 per room for ceilings.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
