Photography quote calculator for Wix

Show session prices by type, extra hours, second shooter and delivery, and collect enquiries that already include what the client wants.

Built for: Wedding, portrait, family, event and commercial photographers and videographers.

What your visitors answer

  • Type of session: Portrait, Family, Wedding, Event, Product / commercial
  • Extra hours beyond the package (h)
  • Photographers: One, Two (second shooter)
  • Delivery: Online gallery, Gallery + prints, Gallery + album
  • Travel distance (miles)
  • Rush delivery (48 hours)

How the sample prices work

The photographer template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Type of session: Portrait adds $180; Family adds $250; Wedding adds $1,800; Event adds $600; Product / commercial adds $400
  • Extra hours beyond the package: $150 per h
  • Photographers: Two (second shooter) adds $300
  • Delivery: Gallery + prints adds $150; Gallery + album adds $400
  • Travel distance: $0.80 per miles
  • Rush delivery (48 hours): adds 20%
  • Minimum total: $150

Pricing tips for photography businesses

  • Show a package price per session type and charge extra hours separately. Clients compare packages before they contact you.
  • Use conditions to show the second-shooter question only for weddings and events, so portrait clients are not asked about it.
  • Add a rush-delivery option as a percentage so it scales with the size of the job.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the photographer template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can some questions appear only for certain session types?

Yes. Every field can have show-when conditions that look at earlier answers.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
