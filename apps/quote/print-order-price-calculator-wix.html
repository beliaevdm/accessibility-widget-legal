How the sample prices work

The print shop template ships with these sample prices. Every number can be changed in the dashboard.

  • Quantity: volume tiers, $0.30 up to 100, $0.18 up to 500, $0.10 up to 2000, $0.07 above

Pricing tips for printing businesses

  • Use volume tiers on the quantity field: the price per piece falls as the run grows, which is how print is normally priced.
  • Add per-unit options for paper, lamination and double-sided printing so each choice scales with the quantity.
  • Keep a setup fee and a minimum order so tiny runs stay worthwhile.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the print shop template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

What is the difference between volume and graduated tiers?

With volume tiers the tier a quantity falls in prices every unit. With graduated tiers each slice of the quantity uses its own price.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
