Pricing tips for agency businesses

  • Show a budget range. Website projects change after discovery and a range filters out unrealistic expectations early.
  • Price pages per unit and features as add-ons so the client sees what drives the cost.
  • Add a timeline uplift for fast and urgent projects to reflect the pressure on your schedule.

Add it to your Wix site

Install Instant Quote Calculator from the Wix App Market, pick the web design agency template in the app dashboard, adjust the prices, then drag the widget onto a page in the Wix editor and choose the calculator in its settings. Requests arrive in the dashboard and in your Wix contacts.

Questions

Can the calculator hide the price until the visitor leaves details?

Yes. Choose "Ask for contact details to reveal the price" in the Leads tab to use it as a lead-generation form.

Do I need a paid plan?

No. The Free plan includes one live calculator with up to eight fields, every template, conditions, the requests list, Wix contacts sync and dashboard notifications. Premium adds unlimited calculators, custom formulas, webhooks, CSV export and custom colors.

Where do the requests go?

Each request is saved with its price breakdown in the app dashboard, added to your Wix contacts, and announced in your Wix dashboard and the Wix Owner app.

Is the price a visitor sees binding?

No. It is an estimate. Your own terms and your final quote decide what is binding.
