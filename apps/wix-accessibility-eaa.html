Wix accessibility: EAA, WCAG and ADA basics for your site

A practical overview for owners of small Wix sites. It is general information, not legal advice.

What the EAA changes

The European Accessibility Act (EAA) has applied since 28 June 2025 to many products and services offered to consumers in the EU, including e-commerce. In practice, sites that sell to EU customers are expected to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Check with a qualified adviser whether and how it applies to your business.

What a visitor toolbar can and cannot do

  • Can: let visitors enlarge text, raise contrast, use a dyslexia-friendly font, highlight links, enlarge the cursor, pause animations, and apply one-click profiles.
  • Cannot: fix underlying problems such as missing alt text, unlabeled form fields, or keyboard traps. A toolbar is one helpful layer, not a substitute for an accessibility audit.

A short checklist for Wix owners

  • Add alt text to meaningful images.
  • Keep sufficient colour contrast and real heading structure.
  • Make sure every form field has a label.
  • Publish an accessibility statement with a contact address for feedback.
  • Offer visitors display controls (toolbar).
