## 3. Information the app processes

**Comparisons created by the site owner:** links to the before and after photos (from the site's Wix media, or https
links the site owner pastes), descriptions for screen readers, captions, labels, design settings and a name visible
only to the site owner. The app reads the site's plan from Wix to know which features are available.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. Moving the slider happens only in the visitor's
browser.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser the widget uses `sessionStorage` to keep a copy of the comparison for faster
loading; it is cleared when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. Photos load from the site's own Wix media or from the https addresses the site owner entered. The
sample comparison shown the first time the app is opened uses two illustrations hosted on GitHub Pages; it is shown
only in the dashboard, never to visitors.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Comparisons stay in the site's Wix collections until the site owner deletes them in the app dashboard or in the site's CMS.
Photos stay in the site's media library; the app never deletes them.
