## 3. Information the app processes

**FAQs added by the site owner:** questions, answers and categories typed, pasted or imported, and the FAQ's
settings (layout, theme, colours and texts). The FAQ's name is visible only to the site owner.

**Visitors:** the app has no forms and collects **no personal data from visitors**. Searching and opening questions
happen in the visitor's browser and are not recorded.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to keep a copy of the FAQ for faster loading; it is
cleared when the tab is closed. "Copy link" writes a link to the clipboard only when the visitor clicks it.
In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. From visitors' browsers it loads only images the site owner puts in answers; links open only when a
visitor clicks them. The widget adds FAQ structured data (schema.org) so that search engines can read the questions
and answers already shown on the page.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

FAQs stay in the site's Wix collections until the site owner deletes them in the app dashboard or in the site's CMS. Removing
the widget from a page does not delete the FAQ.
