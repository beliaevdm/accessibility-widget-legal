## 3. Information the app processes

**Generators (entered by the site owner):** templates, images, default captions, watermark text or logo, blocked
words, texts and appearance settings. Stored in the site's Wix collections.

**Making a meme:** the meme is drawn entirely in the visitor's browser. Captions a visitor types and photos they pick
from their own device are **never uploaded** to the app's server or to the site. Downloading, sharing and copying
happen on the visitor's device.

**Email before download (only if the site owner turns it on):** the email address, the name if the site owner asks
for it, and which template was used. The app also adds the visitor to the site owner's Wix Contacts.

**Usage counts:** anonymous counters per generator and day (views, memes started, downloads, shares and how often each
template was used). They contain no personal data and no meme content.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to keep a copy of the generator for faster loading, to
count a view only once per session and to remember that the visitor already left an email in this session; it is
cleared when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether the sample content was already added and whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. Its fonts are bundled, not loaded from font services. Template images load from the addresses the
site owner chose, usually the site's own Wix media. When a visitor taps Share, their device's own share sheet opens;
nothing is sent until the visitor picks an app. If the site itself already uses Google Tag Manager, the widget adds
`meme_download` and `meme_share` events to the page's data layer in the visitor's browser.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Generators, leads and counters stay in the site's Wix collections until the site owner deletes them. Leads can be
deleted in the Leads tab of the app dashboard or in the site's CMS; deleting a generator does not delete the leads it
collected. Contacts added to Wix Contacts are managed there by the site owner.
