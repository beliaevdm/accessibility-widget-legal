# Privacy Policy — Polls & Surveys

_Last updated: September 2026_

---

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Polls & Surveys app ("we", "our", "the app")
handles information when it is installed on a Wix website. The app is developed and
operated by an independent developer and is distributed through the Wix App Market.

Contact: bieliaiev.d@gmail.com

---

## 2. Roles

The **site owner** who installs the app is the data controller for the poll
content they create and the responses they collect from their visitors. We act as
a data processor, operating the app's backend on the site owner's behalf. All
poll data is stored in the site owner's own Wix Data collections on Wix
infrastructure and is subject to [Wix's Privacy Policy](https://www.wix.com/about/privacy).

---

## 3. What Data the App Processes

**Poll content (entered by the site owner):** questions, options, images,
thank-you messages, redirect URLs, appearance settings, and — on the paid plan —
saved themes and custom CSS. Stored in the site's Wix Data collections.

**Votes (submitted by site visitors):** the selected option(s) or ranking, a
random per-browser token generated by the app, and a **salted, non-reversible
SHA-256 fingerprint** derived from the visitor's IP address. The fingerprint is
salted with the poll's identifier, is used only to stop the same person voting
twice on the same poll, and cannot be reversed to an IP address. **The raw IP
address is never stored.**

**Lead-capture emails (optional):** if the site owner turns on the lead-capture
step, a visitor may enter their email address before or after voting. These
emails are stored in the site owner's Wix Data collection and are visible only to
the site owner, who may export them to CSV. Do not enable this feature unless your
own site privacy policy tells visitors their email will be collected.

**Aggregate results:** vote tallies and a response-over-time count, shown to the
site owner in the dashboard and — as configured by the site owner — to visitors.

---

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. It stores one item in the visitor's browser
`localStorage` (the anonymous vote token) so a visitor is not asked to vote again
on a device where they already voted. This value never leaves the device except
as part of a vote submission.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

When the site owner selects a **custom Google font** for a poll (a paid-plan
feature), the poll widget requests that font file from Google Fonts
(`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`) at page load. This is a standard
web-font request; see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
If no custom Google font is set, no request is made to Google.

The app uses **no analytics, tracking, or advertising services** of its own.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Poll content, votes, and leads persist in the site owner's Wix Data collections
until the site owner deletes the poll (which also deletes its votes and leads) or
uninstalls the app. Site owners can delete any poll at any time from the
dashboard.

---

## 7. GDPR / CCPA

Because the app stores visitor votes and (optionally) visitor emails on the site
owner's behalf, the **site owner** is responsible for having a lawful basis for
that processing and for responding to data-subject requests, using the export and
delete tools in the dashboard. We do not use any of this data for our own
purposes, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties.

If you believe your privacy has been affected by this app, contact the site owner
first; you may also contact us at bieliaiev.d@gmail.com.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children. Site owners should not run polls that
knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the age of digital
consent in their jurisdiction).

reflects the latest revision.
