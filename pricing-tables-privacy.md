## 3. Information the app processes

**Tables (entered by the site owner):** plans, prices, rows, button texts and links, and design settings. Stored in
the site's Wix collections.

**Button clicks:** when a visitor clicks a plan's button, the app stores one record with the table, the plan and the
day. It stores **no personal data** — no name, email, network address, device or location. The site owner sees
totals per plan.

**Visitors:** the app has no forms and collects **no personal data from visitors**.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to keep a copy of the table for faster loading and to
count a click on a plan only once per visit; it is cleared when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. If the site owner picks a Google font for a table, visitors' browsers load it from Google Fonts, which
receives the visitor's network address as with any web request ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
Links on plan buttons open only when a visitor clicks them and are then governed by those sites' own policies. The
widget adds structured data (schema.org) describing the plans already shown on the page so that search engines can
read them.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Tables and click records stay in the site's Wix collections until the site owner deletes them in the app dashboard
or in the site's CMS. Removing the widget from a page does not delete the table.
