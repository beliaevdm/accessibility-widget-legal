## 3. Information the app processes

**Settings chosen by the site owner:** whether the toolbar is on, its colour and position, which tools it offers
and, optionally, a feedback email address shown to visitors. They are stored as the parameters of the app's script
in the site's Wix settings.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. The choices a visitor makes in the toolbar (text
size, contrast, spacing and the other tools) are saved only in that visitor's browser and are never sent to us, to
the site owner or to anyone else — they can say something about a visitor's needs, so they stay on the device.

**Feedback:** if the site owner shows a feedback email address, the "Open email client" button opens the visitor's
own email program addressed to the site owner. We are not part of that message and never receive it.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it keeps the visitor's toolbar choices in `localStorage` so they apply on every
page and the next visit. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown and whether the settings were saved at least once.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. Only when a visitor turns on the dyslexia-friendly font, the browser loads the OpenDyslexic font from
`fonts.cdnfonts.com`; that request is governed by [cdnfonts.com's privacy policy](https://www.cdnfonts.com/privacy-policy).
In the site owner's dashboard, the app reports two events to Wix — the dashboard was opened and the settings were
saved — which Wix shows us only as installation-level statistics.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Settings stay until the site owner changes them or uninstalls the app; uninstalling removes the toolbar from the
site. A visitor's toolbar choices stay in their browser until they press "Reset" in the toolbar or clear the site's
data in their browser.
