## 3. Information the app processes

**Quizzes (entered by the site owner):** questions, answers, results, images, texts, appearance settings and,
optionally, a webhook address. Stored in the site's Wix collections.

**Playing a quiz:** the visitor's answers are processed in their browser to show the result. When the visitor
finishes, the answers are sent to the app's server only to update anonymous counters; they are not stored with any
identifier.

**Leads (only if the site owner turns on the lead form):** the name and email address the visitor typed (only the
fields the site owner asks for), the visitor's answers and result, which the app's server calculates from the quiz.

**Contacts:** the app adds the visitor's name and email address to the site owner's Wix Contacts so the lead
appears with the owner's other customers.

**Webhooks (only if the site owner enters a webhook address):** each lead is also sent to that address; the site
owner chooses that service and is responsible for it.

**Usage counts:** anonymous counters per quiz and day (views, starts, completions, how often each result was
reached and how often each question was answered correctly). They contain no personal data.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to keep a copy of the quiz for faster loading and to
count a view only once per session; it is cleared when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether the sample content was already added and whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. Images in a quiz load from the addresses the site owner chose. Share buttons are plain links to
Facebook, X and WhatsApp that open only when a visitor clicks them. If the site itself already uses Google Tag
Manager, Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel, the quiz passes them events about its own use inside the visitor's
browser; it loads none of these services itself.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Quizzes, leads and counters stay in the site's Wix collections until the site owner deletes them. Leads can be
deleted in the Leads tab of the app dashboard or in the site's CMS; deleting a quiz does not delete the leads it collected.
Contacts added to Wix Contacts are managed there by the site owner.
