## 3. Information the app processes

**Settings chosen by the site owner:** the effects, colours, dates, page addresses, campaign names (visible only to
the site owner) and, if chosen, emoji or images from the site's own media. They are stored in the parameters of the
app's script in the site's Wix settings. The app reads the site's address and plan from Wix to check where the effect
may show and which features are included.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. The effect is drawn in the visitor's browser from
the settings delivered with the page; nothing about the visitor is sent to us or anyone else.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to remember, for the current tab, that the visitor
stopped the animation and that a "once per visit" effect has already played; both are cleared when the tab is
closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services** of its own. Images chosen for an effect load from the site's own Wix media.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Settings stay until the site owner changes them. Turning the effect off removes the app's script from the site;
uninstalling the app removes the script and its settings.
