# Privacy Policy — Seasonal Effects

_Last updated: October 2026_

---

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Seasonal Effects app ("we", "our", "the app") handles information when it is installed on a Wix website. The app is developed and operated by an independent developer and is distributed through the Wix App Market.

Contact: bieliaiev.d@gmail.com

---

## 2. Roles

The **site owner** who installs the app decides which effects show on their site. The app's settings are stored in the site's own embedded-script settings on Wix infrastructure and are subject to [Wix's Privacy Policy](https://www.wix.com/about/privacy). We do not keep a copy of them on servers of our own.

---

## 3. What Data the App Processes

**Settings chosen by the site owner:** the effects, colours, dates, page addresses, campaign names (visible only to the site owner) and, if chosen, emoji or images from the site's own media. The app reads the site's address and plan from Wix to check where the effect may show and which features are included.

**Visitors:** the app collects **no personal data from visitors**. The effect is drawn in the visitor's browser from the settings delivered with the page; nothing about the visitor is sent to us or to anyone else.

---

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

The app **does not set any cookies**. In the visitor's browser it uses `sessionStorage` to remember two choices of that visitor for the current tab: that they stopped the animation, and that a "once per visit" effect has already played. Both are cleared when the tab is closed. In the site owner's dashboard, `localStorage` remembers whether a short review request was already shown.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app uses **no analytics, tracking or advertising services**. Images chosen for an effect are loaded from the site's own Wix media.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Settings persist until the site owner changes them or uninstalls the app. Turning the effect off removes the app's script from the site; uninstalling the app removes it and its settings.

---

## 7. GDPR / CCPA

The app processes no personal data of visitors. We do not sell or share any data and do not use the site owner's settings for our own purposes.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from visitors.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
